Concrete Inspection & Advisory Workflow

Four-stage methodology integrating image suitability screening, ML classification, and rehabilitation guidelines.

01

Image Suitability Check

Validates file format (JPG/PNG), screens out non-concrete objects (people, vehicles, food, furniture), and verifies aggregate texture.

02

AI Model Classification

Ensemble classifier predicts defect among 6 categories or verifies sound concrete condition with genuine confidence scores.

03

Repair & Prevention Facts

Presents root causes, preliminary severity rating, prevention practices, suitable repair methods, and maintenance advice.

04

RCC Slab Strengthening

Links observed structural distress to the comprehensive 7-step RCC slab strengthening and MS section installation method.
